## Conclusion
1. Headroom OPT vs LRU on the only complete trace: -41% (20 GiB) / -49% (17.17 GiB) reads, but that trace is a cold start, and the OPT floor is the compulsory count. 51-59% of LRU's reads are compulsory (never in RAM in this run), 41-44% are cold-start evictions of never-used residents, only 0-5% are genuine capacity re-references.
2. Realistic online policies capture ~20% of reads, and ~all of it is a startup effect (profile-rank order for never-touched slots instead of expert-id order). It matters after every restart (about 2 tok/s est. in the first 1024 tokens), and fades once the arena is touched. Steady-state benefit of LRU-2/ARC/decayed-freq/TinyLFU: not shown (<=1% apart from the start-up fix on this trace).
3. Admission filters (2nd-miss) are net harmful on first-touch-heavy traffic (+15-23% reads) and break the immediate-admission property the user relies on; do not use. TinyLFU degenerates to LRU here.
4. Because compulsory misses dominate and live per-request reads stay at ~1-5k blobs even when warm, the larger lever is not eviction but hiding/avoiding first-touch reads (profile refresh from Hermes+English traffic, cross-layer prefetch like Fate). Eviction policy is not the first thing to build.
5. Not answerable yet: steady-state, multi-trajectory headroom, per-request worst case, Chinese-vs-English start effect, 4096-token capacity effect. They need the blocked warm capture.
